Hi, I'm Diego, known online as **dieghernan**. This is where I share my interest in R, spatial data, cartography and open-source software.

## Maps, R and open source

Much of my work revolves around making geographic data easier to access, explore and visualize in R. My projects include [tidyterra](https://dieghernan.github.io/tidyterra/), for working with terra spatial data using tidyverse methods and ggplot2, and [giscoR](https://ropengov.github.io/giscoR/), [mapSpain](https://ropenspain.github.io/mapSpain/) and [geobounds](https://dieghernan.github.io/geobounds/), for accessing geographic boundaries and mapping data.

I also work on tools beyond cartography, such as [cffr](https://docs.ropensci.org/cffr/) for software citation and [resmush](https://dieghernan.github.io/resmush/) for image optimization.

## About One World

One World brings together my projects, maps and things I learn along the way. On the blog, I share examples and experiments with R, often starting with a dataset, a visual idea or a map I want to recreate.
